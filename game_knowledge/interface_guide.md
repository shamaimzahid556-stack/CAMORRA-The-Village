# Interface guide

## Setup screen
Choose the TOTAL CHARACTERS ({{min_players}} to {{max_players}}), type a unique name for every player, and choose how many MAFIA there are ({{min_mafia}} to {{max_mafia}}). The Detective and Doctor counts are fixed at one each, and the VILLAGERS count updates automatically. A line below shows whether the setup is valid. START THE GAME is disabled when the setup is invalid. Error messages such as "Player names must be unique" appear on screen.

## Role assignment screens
"PASS THE DEVICE" names the next player. SHOW MY ROLE reveals the role card to that player. HIDE MY ROLE hides it and passes to the next player. CONTINUE appears on the pass screen between players. After the last player, the Night 1 introduction appears with the roster of players.

## Night introduction and pass screens
BEGIN NIGHT starts the sequence of night turns. Each turn begins with a "PASS THE DEVICE" screen that names the player, and I'M READY reveals that player's action screen. Press I'M READY only when the named player is holding the device.

## Night action screen
The top bar shows the night number, the player's name and a badge with their role. The main area shows the instruction and the choices, such as player cards or activities. Tap a card to select it, and a CONTINUE button appears. Nothing is submitted until CONTINUE is pressed. Mafia shortlist selection has a CONTINUE button that stays disabled until enough players are selected.

## Detective private result screen
After the Detective investigates someone, a card says MAFIA or INNOCENT and tells the Detective to keep it private. HIDE RESULT & CONTINUE hides the card and passes the device on.

## Morning screen
The morning screen shows the results of the night. BEGIN DISCUSSION continues to the discussion. If the game has ended, the button says VIEW GAME RESULT.

## Discussion screen
The discussion screen shows the day number and a countdown timer. START VOTING moves on to voting immediately. When the timer reaches zero, voting starts by itself.

## Voting screens
A "PASS THE DEVICE" screen names the voter, with the voter's position, such as "voter 2 of 6". I'M READY opens the voting screen, which lists the players the voter may eliminate. Tap a player and press CONTINUE to submit.

## Elimination screen
The elimination screen announces the eliminated player and their actual role, and mentions a tie if there was one. BEGIN NEXT NIGHT starts the next night. VIEW GAME RESULT appears if the game has ended.

## Game-over screen
The game-over screen announces the winning team and lists every player with their role and status. NEW GAME reloads the game so a new one can be set up.

## Sound button
A small speaker button in the bottom-left corner turns the game sounds on or off. The sounds are dark on purpose, to make the village feel haunted. The front page and the role assignment have a low drone, cold wind, whispers and distant wolves. The night has crickets, owls and wolves, and a deep bell when it begins. The morning and the discussion have eerie wind, crows and a low bell. The voting and the elimination have a heartbeat. Every button makes a dark knock, and SHOW MY ROLE makes a sting. When the Civilians win, joyful music plays. When the Mafia wins, dark music plays with evil laughter and wolves. The setting is remembered in the browser. A browser only allows sound after you have interacted with the page once, for example by pressing any button. The sound button is not part of the game rules.

## About the AI Companion
The round button with the robot picture in the bottom-right corner opens the AI Companion, a helper that explains the rules, roles and screens. Its chat is private. It starts empty each time it is opened, it is cleared when you close it with the X, and it is also cleared when the game moves to another screen or player. It does not appear on the final result screen. It cannot tell you any other player's role or secret choice.

## Chat settings and answer styles
The gear icon in the header of the AI Companion opens the chat settings, where you choose how the helper talks to you. The styles are: {{answer_styles}}. Friendly is the default. Personal talks to you directly. Robotic answers like a machine. Beginner uses very simple words. Concise gives the shortest answer. Detailed gives full step-by-step explanations. Noir narrator sounds dark and atmospheric. A style only changes the tone and length of the answers. It never changes the rules, and it never lets the helper reveal anyone's secret role. Only your chosen style is remembered in the browser, never the chat itself.

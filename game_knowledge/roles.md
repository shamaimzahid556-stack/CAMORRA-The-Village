# Roles

## Role overview
CAMORRA has four roles: Mafia, Detective, Doctor and Villager. The Mafia belong to the Mafia team. The Detective, the Doctor and the Villagers belong to the Town team. The Mafia, the Detective and the Doctor have a night action. Villagers have no special power, although they still take a night turn.

Simple version: Mafia are the bad guys. Detective, Doctor and Villager are the good guys. Only the Mafia, Detective and Doctor have real powers at night.

## Mafia
The Mafia team's goal is to take over the village. Each night, the Mafia secretly chooses one living non-Mafia player to kill. Mafia players know who their Mafia partners are, because the partners are shown on the role card, and each Mafia player takes their own night turn in the normal order. A Mafia player cannot choose another Mafia member as a night target.

By day, a Mafia player discusses and votes like everyone else, except that the vote list never offers their Mafia partners. If there are two or more living Mafia, the first living Mafia player builds a shortlist of {{shortlist_min}} to {{shortlist_max}} possible victims, and then each Mafia player picks one target from that shortlist. The target with the most Mafia votes dies, and a tie is decided at random.

The Mafia's night kill fails if the Doctor protected that same player.

Simple version: at night the Mafia secretly pick someone to kill. By day they act innocent and try not to be voted out. They know each other, but nobody else knows them.

## Detective
The Detective is on the Town team. Each night the Detective investigates one living player other than themselves. The Detective immediately sees a private result on screen: either MAFIA or INNOCENT. That result is private, and the screen asks the Detective not to show it to anyone.

If the Detective investigates a Mafia member, that Mafia member is eliminated at the end of that night, and the morning report announces which player the Detective found. If the result is INNOCENT, nothing about it is announced. The Detective's identity is never announced by the game.

Simple version: every night the Detective checks one person and learns if they are Mafia or innocent. If it is a Mafia member, that player is removed by morning.

## Doctor
The Doctor is on the Town team. Each night the Doctor chooses one living player to protect. If the Mafia targets the protected player, that player does not die. The Doctor may protect any living player, including themselves, but the self-save can be used only {{doctor_self_saves}} time in the whole game. Once it has been used, the self-save option is gone and the Doctor must protect someone else.

The morning report names the player the Doctor chose to protect. It does not say who the Doctor is.

Simple version: every night the Doctor picks one person to protect from the Mafia. They can protect themselves once per game.

## Villager
A Villager is on the Town team and has no special power. Villagers still take a night turn: they choose one of {{villager_activity_count}} activities ({{villager_activities}}). The activity has no effect on the game.

A Villager's strength is their observations, their discussion and their vote.

Simple version: Villagers have no power. They watch, talk and vote. Their night choice does nothing.

## Town team and Mafia team
The Town team is made up of the Detective, the Doctor and the Villagers, and the result screen calls the winners "Civilians". The Mafia team is made up of all the Mafia. A player's team is decided by their role and never changes during the game.

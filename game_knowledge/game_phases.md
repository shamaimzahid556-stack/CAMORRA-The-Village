# Game phases

## Order of the phases
A game moves through these phases in order: setup, role assignment, then repeating cycles of night, morning report, discussion, voting and elimination. After role assignment the game goes to Night 1. When a vote ends without a winner, the elimination screen is followed by the next night. When either side wins, the game moves to the game-over screen.

Simple version: setup, roles, then night, morning, discussion, vote, elimination, night and so on, until somebody wins.

## Role assignment phase
Role assignment happens once, at the start. The screen shows "PASS THE DEVICE" and the name of one player. Only that player should look. The player presses SHOW MY ROLE and sees a private role card with their role and a short description. Mafia players also see their Mafia partners on the card. The player then presses HIDE MY ROLE, and the device is passed to the next player, who presses CONTINUE and then SHOW MY ROLE. After the last player has seen their role, the game shows the Night 1 introduction.

Simple version: pass the device around. Each person taps SHOW MY ROLE, reads it privately, taps HIDE MY ROLE, and passes it on.

## Night phase
Night is a sequence of private turns. After the night introduction screen (BEGIN NIGHT), each living player takes a turn in the order the names were entered in setup. For each turn the game shows a "PASS THE DEVICE" screen with the player's name. When the player presses I'M READY, their private action screen appears. When the last living player has finished, the night is resolved and the morning report is shown.

Simple version: at night everyone takes a quick private turn, one at a time. The game then works out what happened.

## Day: morning report
The morning screen shows the results of the night: who the Mafia targeted and whether that player survived, which player the Doctor protected, and what happened with the Detective (a found Mafia member, the Detective still working, or the Detective out of the game). The BEGIN DISCUSSION button continues to the discussion. If the night ended the game, the button says VIEW GAME RESULT instead.

## Day: discussion
During discussion the players talk to each other in person. The screen shows a countdown timer that starts at {{discussion_minutes}} minutes. The game does not record or judge the conversation. When the timer ends, voting starts automatically. The players can also press START VOTING at any time to start earlier.

Simple version: you have {{discussion_minutes}} minutes to talk face to face. Press START VOTING when you are ready to vote, or wait for the timer.

## Day: voting
Voting is private and happens one living player at a time. Each living player gets a "PASS THE DEVICE" screen, presses I'M READY, picks one living player to eliminate and presses CONTINUE. After the last living player has voted, the votes are counted.

## Day: elimination
The elimination screen announces which player was eliminated and reveals that player's actual role. If there was a tie, a message says so. BEGIN NEXT NIGHT starts the next night. If the elimination ended the game, the button says VIEW GAME RESULT instead.

## Eliminated players
An eliminated or killed player is out of the game. They no longer take night turns and no longer vote. Their name stays in the player roster, marked OUT.

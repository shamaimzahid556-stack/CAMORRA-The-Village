# Voting and elimination

## How the voting system works (overview)
Voting happens every day after the discussion. Each living player votes once, privately, in turn on the shared device, for a living player other than themselves. Nobody can skip, and nobody sees who voted for whom. When everyone has voted, the player with the most votes is eliminated; if there is a tie, the game picks randomly among the tied players. The elimination screen then shows the eliminated player's real role.

Simple version: everyone votes secretly in turn, the most votes goes out, and a tie is settled randomly by the game.

## Who sees my vote? Are votes private?
Votes are private, secret and anonymous, not open or public. The game does not show who voted for whom to anyone, not during voting and not afterwards. The elimination screen shows only who was eliminated and their role.

Simple version: nobody sees your vote.

## How voting starts
Voting starts when someone presses START VOTING on the discussion screen, or automatically when the {{discussion_minutes}}-minute discussion timer reaches zero. The game then shows a "PASS THE DEVICE" screen for the first living player.

Simple version: finish talking, press START VOTING, and then each player votes privately in turn.

## How a player casts a vote
Each living player votes once, in turn, in the order the names were entered. The named player presses I'M READY, sees a list of players they may vote for, taps one player and presses CONTINUE. The vote is recorded and the device is passed to the next living player. The game does not show who voted for whom to anyone.

## Who can be voted for
A player can vote only for a living player other than themselves. A Mafia player's list also leaves out their Mafia partners, because Mafia cannot vote to eliminate a partner. If the game refuses a vote, it shows a message on screen explaining why.

## No skip or abstain option: what happens if a player does not vote
Every living player must cast a vote. The interface has no skip or abstain button, and eliminated players do not vote. Voting cannot finish until every living player has voted, so if a player does not vote, the game simply waits for that vote.

## How votes are counted and ties
After the last living player has voted, the game counts the votes. The player with the most votes is eliminated. If two or more players are tied for the most votes, the game picks one of the tied players at random, and the elimination screen says that there was a tie and that the computer moderator chose randomly.

Simple version: most votes goes out. In a tie the computer picks randomly between the tied players.

## What happens after the vote
The elimination screen names the eliminated player and shows their actual role. The eliminated player is out of the game. The game then checks whether a team has won. If nobody has won, BEGIN NEXT NIGHT starts the next night. If a team has won, the button reads VIEW GAME RESULT.

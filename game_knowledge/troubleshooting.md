# Troubleshooting and beginner help

## I do not understand my role
Your role card at role assignment describes your role in a sentence. Mafia know their partners. Detective investigates one player per night. Doctor protects one player per night, with one self-save per game. Villagers have no power. If you are unsure, ask the AI Companion to explain a role in general terms. It can describe roles, but it cannot tell you what anyone else is.

## I do not know what to do at night
At night you only act on your own turn. Wait for the "PASS THE DEVICE" screen that shows your name, press I'M READY, and then do the action for your role: the Mafia pick a victim, the Doctor picks someone to protect, the Detective picks someone to investigate, and a Villager picks any activity. Press CONTINUE to finish.

## The game says it is someone else's turn
Night actions and votes happen in a fixed order, and the game rejects actions from a player whose turn it is not. A message names the player whose turn it is. Pass the device to that player.

## I am confused about voting
After the discussion, every living player votes privately, one at a time. Pick one living player other than yourself and press CONTINUE. The player with the most votes is eliminated, and a tie is broken randomly by the computer. There is no skip button.

## A button is disabled or an action is unavailable
When you cannot use your role's ability, or a button looks unavailable, check these reasons. A disabled (greyed-out) button cannot be pressed until its condition is met. START THE GAME is disabled while the setup is invalid, for example when there would be no Villager. The Mafia shortlist CONTINUE button stays disabled until at least {{shortlist_min}} players are selected. The Doctor cannot choose themselves after the one self-save has been used. The Detective cannot investigate themselves. A Mafia player cannot choose or vote for a Mafia partner. Nobody can vote for themselves.

## What happens after I choose something
Nothing changes immediately. At night, your choice is stored and the device is passed on. The results appear together in the morning report after everyone has finished. After a vote, the results appear on the elimination screen after the last player votes.

## I mixed up my own role and what another player might be
Rules and role descriptions are general knowledge, and the AI Companion can explain them. Who actually holds a role is secret. The AI Companion does not know other players' roles and will not guess or confirm them.

## I do not know which phase the game is in
The game is in role assignment if you see role cards, in the night if you see "PASS THE DEVICE" with a night number, in the morning or discussion if you see the results or a timer, in voting if you see "voter number of total", and in elimination if a player has just been removed.

## I refreshed the page
Refreshing the browser does not lose the game. The server keeps it, and the page returns to the screen the game was on: the pass-the-device screen of the player whose turn it is, the discussion timer with its remaining time, the voting pass screen or the elimination screen. A player who was looking at a private role card or a night action screen is shown the pass-the-device screen again, so they press SHOW MY ROLE or I'M READY once more. Pressing START THE GAME replaces the game in progress with a new one.

## An error message appeared
Red banner messages explain what the game refused, such as "It is currently Sam's night turn", "You cannot vote for yourself", "Mafia cannot choose another Mafia member" or "Player names must be unique". Fix the choice that the message describes and try again.

## I cannot hear any sound
Check that the speaker button in the bottom-left corner is not showing the muted symbol, and that the volume of your device and browser tab is up. A browser only plays sound after you have interacted with the page once, so press any button and the sounds will start. Sounds are not part of the game rules, and the game works the same without them.
